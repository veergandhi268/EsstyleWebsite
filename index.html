Precision Customization

Advanced Branding & Embellishment Technologies

Your logo is your identity. At ESSTYLE, we deploy state-of-the-art Japanese embroidery machinery and digital hybrid printers to ensure sharp rendering, rich texture, and enduring vibrancy.

High-Density Embroidery

Precision multi-head Tajima embroidery with German Madeira threads. Delivers rich 3D texture and prestigious corporate sophistication that never fades.

  • Best for: Corporate Polos, Blazers & Caps
  • 100% Wash & Bleach Proof
  • Metallic Gold/Silver thread options

High-Definition DTF / Hybrid

Direct-to-Film digital technology delivering photographic detail, vibrant gradients, and ultra-crisp micro-text on both cotton and synthetic blends with a soft-hand feel.

  • Best for: Intricate logos & multi-color art
  • Ultra-stretch & zero surface cracking
  • Fast turnaround on pilot batches

All-Over Dye Sublimation

Molecular heat-transfer dye technology where colors fuse directly into polymer fibres. Completely breathable, zero weight, and guaranteed never to crack or peel.

  • Best for: Sports Jerseys & Event Activewear
  • Unlimited colors & edge-to-edge prints
  • 100% Breathable & Lightweight

High-Volume Screen Printing

Traditional plastisol and eco-friendly water-based discharge screen printing. Extremely cost-effective for large bulk orders while maintaining punchy solid colors.

  • Best for: Bulk Promo & Factory Staff Tees
  • Maximum economy on 200+ units
  • Eco-friendly non-toxic inks
